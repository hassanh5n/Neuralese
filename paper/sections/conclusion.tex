\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

\GST{} feeds a sampled token plus a residual.
In a 1.5B reasoning model on GSM8K, we tested whether that residual acts as a token-level superposition. % SETUP
A trace of the runner-up persists, mostly in early-layer keys and values, and the model reads the residual, but
accuracy does not change detectably and the real residual changes answers no more often than a random one.
The superposition is present and read, but we find no evidence that it is used.
Testing learned superposition in trained models, and superposition of whole reasoning paths, with the same
shared-noise control is the clearest next step.
